💾 Data Storage Info

          

            Where is your data stored?

            All store data is saved in your browser's localStorage — on this device and browser only.
          

          

            

              📱
              
This device only
Data does not sync between devices. If you log in from a different phone or tablet, it will have no data unless you restore a backup.

            

            

              🔄
              
Survives page refresh
Closing and reopening the browser is safe — your data and session will be restored automatically.

            

            

              🚫
              
Incognito / Private mode
Data is lost when the tab closes. Always use a regular browser window.

            

            

              🧹
              
Clearing browser data
If you clear your browser's cache, cookies, or site data — all POS data will be erased. Always download a backup first.

            

            

              ☁️
              
No cloud, no internet required
This POS works fully offline. Nothing is sent to any server. Your data never leaves this device.

            

          

          

            
🛡 Best practice

            
Download a Full Backup (JSON) daily and save it to Google Drive or email it to yourself. If something goes wrong, use Restore to recover all your data.
